# Style Guide Examples

## Email Subject Lines

| ❌ Before                                        | ✅ After                              | Rule Applied                         |
|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| IMPORTANT UPDATE REGARDING YOUR ACCOUNT!!!      | Your account settings have changed   | No ALL CAPS, no "!!!", state the fact |
| Newsletter #12                                  | 3 ways to save time this week        | Benefit-led, not a label             |
| Act now: free upgrade expires soon              | Your upgrade is ready until Friday   | No spam words or fake urgency        |
| Re: Information Pertaining To Your Recent Order | Your order #4821 has shipped         | Sentence case, under 50 characters   |
| We Are Excited To Announce Our New Feature      | Meet reports: your data in one view  | Lead with the benefit                |

## Email Body Rewrites

**❌ Before**

> Dear Valued Customer,
>
> We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed successfully by our dedicated
> support team. Please be advised that should you require any further assistance, you may utilize
> the help center, which can be accessed by clicking here.
>
> Best regards,
> Customer Support Department

**✅ After**

> Hi Alex,
>
> Good news: we've processed your request, and you're all set.
>
> Need anything else? **Visit the help center** or just reply to this email.
>
> Thanks,
> Maria from the Support team

**Changes**: removed filler ("we are pleased to inform you", "please be advised"), switched to active
voice and contractions, replaced "utilize"/"click here" with a verb-first CTA, used a real name in the sign-off.

## Common Jargon → Plain English

| Jargon                          | Plain English            |
|---------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Utilize                         | Use                      |
| Leverage                        | Use / build on           |
| Facilitate                      | Help / make easier       |
| Synergy                         | Working together         |
| Going forward                   | From now on              |
| Reach out                       | Contact / email / call   |
| At this point in time           | Now                      |
| In order to                     | To                       |
| We are pleased to inform you    | Good news: / (cut it)    |
| Please be advised               | (cut it)                 |
| Best-in-class solution          | (say what it does)       |
| Circle back                     | Follow up                |

## Social Media Rewrites

### LinkedIn

**❌ Before**

> We are excited to announce that our company has launched a new best-in-class reporting solution
> which leverages cutting-edge technology to facilitate data-driven decision making for enterprises.
> #Reporting #Data #Innovation #Tech #SaaS #Enterprise #Analytics #AI #Growth

**✅ After**

> How long does your team spend building weekly reports? For most teams we talk to, it's 4+ hours.
>
> Our new reports pull your data into one view, so you can spot trends in minutes and spend the rest
> of your week acting on them.
>
> Try it free for 14 days (link in comments).
>
> #Reporting #Analytics #Productivity

**Changes**: opened with a question hook instead of "We are excited to announce", replaced jargon with a
concrete benefit, added a single CTA, cut hashtags from 9 to 3 and moved them to the end.

### Twitter / X

**❌ Before**

> 🚀🚀🚀 HUGE NEWS!!! Our amazing new feature is finally here and you are going to LOVE it, check it out now!!! #launch #news #feature #product

**✅ After**

> New: reports that build themselves. Connect your data once and get a weekly summary in your inbox. 📊 #Analytics

**Changes**: removed ALL CAPS and extra exclamation marks, led with the actual feature, used one emoji
and one hashtag, kept it under 200 characters.

## Support Reply Rewrites

**❌ Before**

> Unfortunately, as per our policy, we are unable to process refunds for accounts where the user
> failed to cancel before the renewal date. Please be advised that the cancellation option is
> located in the account settings.

**✅ After**

> Sorry about the surprise charge. Let's sort this out together.
>
> I've flagged your renewal for a one-time review, and you'll hear back within 24 hours. To stop
> future renewals:
>
> 1. Go to **Settings** > **Billing**.
> 2. Click **Cancel subscription**.
> 3. Confirm on the next screen.
>
> If anything looks off, just reply here and I'll take another look.

**Changes**: led with empathy instead of "Unfortunately", removed blame ("the user failed to") and
"as per our policy", gave the solution first, turned the instructions into numbered steps with bold
UI elements, closed with an offer of further help.
